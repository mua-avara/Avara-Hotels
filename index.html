How to get to Hoarafushi

  1. 1

    Fly to Malé

    Most international flights arrive at Velana International Airport (MLE).

  2. 2

    Fly to Hoarafushi

    Domestic flights with Maldivian take about 45 minutes. They do not run every day, so check the schedule before you book.

  3. 3

    We meet you

    Hoarafushi has its own airport (HRF) on the island. We pick you up by vehicle, and it is about 10 minutes to Avara Sands. Send us your flight details and we will arrange it.

Leave a comfortable gap between your international arrival and your domestic flight, and tell us your flight details so we can plan your pickup. Not sure which flight to take? Message us and we will help.

Guesthouse essentials

Avara Sands sits on a local island, so a few simple customs make your stay smoother for you and for your neighbours.

  • Check-in and check-out

    Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 noon. Need an earlier arrival or later departure? Ask us and we will do our best.

  • Dress code

    Hoarafushi is a local island, home to a community of residents. Away from the beach, wear light clothing that covers your shoulders and knees.

  • Swimwear

    Swimwear is for designated beach areas and for boat trips and excursions. Ask us where to swim, and cover up when you leave the beach.

  • Swimming and weather

    Swimming spots are available in the beach area. Always watch out for bad weather, and follow the advice of our team.

  • Mosques and prayer times

    The island follows the call to prayer. Some shops may close for short periods. Cover fully if you visit a mosque.

  • Alcohol

    Alcohol is prohibited on local islands. Fresh juices, coffee and tea are easy to find, and we are happy to point you to good ones.

  • Photography

    Please ask before photographing people, especially women and children. Boats, reefs and sunsets are always fair game.

  • Respect local life

    Keep noise down in the evening, take litter with you and greet people with a smile. A little courtesy goes a long way.
